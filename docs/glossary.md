# Glossary: the hotel's words

One word for each thing, used on every screen, email, message, the contract and the staff guide.
The code, URLs and database keep their own names (`reservations`, `room_categories`, `/app/reservations`); only what people read follows this page.

Test for any text on screen: would the hotel's best receptionist say these words to a guest or a colleague?

## Bookings and stays

| Say | Not | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **booking** | reservation | A guest's stay, from the moment it's made. "New booking", "Cancel booking", "Bookings" in the menu. |
| **room type** | category | Standard, Deluxe, Suite, Apartment. "Rates & room types". |
| **room** | unit | A numbered room, e.g. Room 204. |
| **guest** | customer, client | The person staying. (Hall bookings have a *customer*.) |
| **website / walk-in / phone / WhatsApp** | source codes | How the booking was made. |
| **held until** *time* | hold expiry, pending | An unpaid booking keeps its room until this time, then it's released. |
| **awaiting payment** | pending payment, pending | Booked but not paid. |
| **confirmed** | | Paid in full; the room is kept. |
| **check in / check out** (verbs), **check-in / check-out** (nouns) | | Arrive and hand over the key / leave. |
| **in house** | checked in (as a list) | Guests staying tonight. |
| **arrivals / departures** | | Due to check in / check out today. |
| **no-show** | | A confirmed guest who didn't arrive. |
| **needs attention** | flagged, attention reason | Something a manager must look at, with the reason shown. |
| **extras** | add-ons | Breakfast, airport pickup and so on, chosen with a booking. |
| **promo code** | discount code, coupon | A code that takes a percentage off the room price. |

## Money

| Say | Not | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **owed** | balance, outstanding, due, amount due | Money the guest still has to pay: "₦5,000 owed". |
| **paid in full** | settled, zero balance | Nothing owed. |
| **take payment** | record payment, collect | The action at the desk: "Take payment · ₦5,000". |
| **payment** | transaction | Money received: cash, transfer, POS, or card online. |
| **cash / transfer / POS / card online** | bank_transfer, pos_terminal, Paystack | How it was paid. |
| **receipt** | invoice | The numbered proof of a payment (RCT-…). |
| **guest bill** | folio, statement | Everything a stay cost and what was paid. |
| **correction** | reversal, adjustment | A manager fixing a payment that was entered wrongly. Payments are never edited or deleted. |
| **refund** | chargeback | Money going back to a guest. Approved by the general manager; paid outside the system. |
| **fee kept** | penalty, cancellation charge | The part kept when a guest cancels late. |

## Rooms

| Say | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **available** | Free tonight and ready. |
| **occupied** | A guest is in it. |
| **needs cleaning** | After check-out, until housekeeping finishes. |
| **ready** | Cleaned and ready for the next guest. |
| **under maintenance** | Out of use, with the reason. |

## Restaurant & bar

| Say | Not | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **till** | POS, point of sale | Where sales are taken. |
| **order** | ticket, tab | What a customer is having. |
| **sale** | transaction | A paid order. |
| **void** | delete, reverse | Cancelling a paid sale; a manager does it, with a reason. |
| **daily closing** | Z-report, end of day | Counting cash against cash sales at the end of the trading day. |
| **trading day** | business day | 5am to 5am, so late-night sales count for the evening they belong to. |
| **received / used / damaged or wasted / count fix** | stock move kinds | What happened to stock. |
| **low** | below reorder level | Stock at or under its "low at" level. |

## People

Owner · General Manager · Hotel Manager · Front Desk · Finance · Restaurant & Bar Manager · Restaurant & Bar Salesperson. "Staff & access" for managing them.
