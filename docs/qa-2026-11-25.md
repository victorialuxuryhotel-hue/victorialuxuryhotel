# QA pass — 25 Nov 2026

Tested on the local dev server (localhost:3000) in the desktop app's browser, at phone (375px) and desktop (1280px) widths.

**Covered:** every public page, the full guest booking flow up to the payment hand-off, guest "My bookings" sign-in, error and not-found pages, design-preview screens.

## Fine — checked and working

- All 12 public pages and the 4 room pages load at phone and desktop width; no broken links on the public site.
- Booking form validation: empty fields show clear messages; local phone format (0803…) is accepted.
- Room hold countdown shows on step 4; "call and quote VL-xxxx" fallback is there.
- "My bookings": doesn't reveal whether an email has bookings (good for privacy).
- Bad booking links show a clean not-found page, not an error.
- Cancellation policy text matches on booking, review and contact pages (24h / 50%).
- `/preview` is switched off in production unless `ENABLE_DESIGN_PREVIEW=true`. Leave it unset.
