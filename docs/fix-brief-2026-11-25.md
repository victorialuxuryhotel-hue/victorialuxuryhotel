# Fix brief — go-live blockers from the 25 Nov QA and system review

You are working on the Victoria Luxury Hotel system (Next.js 16 + D1, see AGENTS.md and README.md). These findings come from hands-on testing of the local build as front desk, bar salesperson and guest, compared with how Mews, Cloudbeds, OPERA (hotel) and Toast, Square, Lightspeed (bar) work. Full write-up: `docs/qa-2026-11-25.md`.

## The principle behind every fix

Every screen must answer, in order:
1. **What is true right now?** Taken from recorded events (checked in, money received, cash counted), never inferred from booking dates or a staff tap.
2. **What needs me?** Everything unfinished whatever its date: overdue, unpaid, not arrived, not counted.
3. **What is the one next action?** One button, in step order. Payment always comes first.

Business rules that must hold (from the contract):
- Everything is paid in full before the guest gets a room or anything else. Nobody owes money.
- The restaurant and bar are independent: customers pay at the counter, nothing is charged to a room.
- Money and activity records are never edited or deleted.

## Fixes, in priority order

Do each as its own commit with tests. Enforce rules in the domain layer and database (triggers), not only in the UI.

### 1. A room is occupied from check-in until check-out
**Now:** Grace Adeyemi (VL-DEMO04) is checked in to Room 107 with check-out Sun 22 Nov. The room board (`server/queries/rooms.ts` `roomBoard`) shows 107 as Available, the room picker on a booking offers 107, and I was able to assign 107 to a new booking (VL-DCBSAU). Same for Funmi in 201. Today's "Rooms right now" says "2 Guests in" while In house lists 4.
**Want:** occupancy = status `checked_in`, regardless of dates, in the board, the picker, the counts, availability for the website, and the DB triggers. The picker never lists an occupied room. Room counts on Today match In house.
**Test:** overstayed checked-in guest → room shows Occupied, is absent from the picker, assigning it via the action fails with a clear message, and the room type's availability on the website drops by one.

### 2. Today shows everything that needs doing (`domain/today.ts` `needsYouNow`, `components/management/today.tsx`)
Add to "Needs you now", each with one button:
- **Overdue check-out**: checked in, check-out date before today → "Grace Adeyemi · due out Sun 22 Nov" → Check out / Change dates.
- **Didn't arrive**: confirmed, check-in before today, not checked in → "Chioma Eze · due Tue 24 Nov" → Mark no-show / Still coming. (These currently appear nowhere on Today and keep holding rooms, e.g. 104/105.)
- **Money owed in house**: checked in with a balance > 0 → first item, "Ibrahim Musa owes ₦1,080,000" → Take payment.
Order: money owed, overdue, didn't arrive, then today's items. "Leaving today" should mark overdue guests clearly rather than mixing them in.
**Test:** unit tests in `domain/today.test.ts` for each case.

### 3. No room before payment; one job per guest
**Now:** an unpaid booking shows two Needs-you-now items ("hasn't paid" and "has no room · Assign"), and the booking page lets staff assign a room while "Awaiting payment", although its own step tracker says "Assign a room · After payment".
**Want:** while a booking is awaiting payment, the only action is Take payment. Hide Assign/Change room until paid; the room is auto-assigned on payment (existing behaviour). Reject assignment on unpaid bookings in the domain (`server/domain/reservations.ts`) and DB.
Also close the known gap: room upgrades (`assignRoom` adding `room_change_kobo`) and staff date extensions (`changeDates`) must be paid before they take effect for checked-in guests.

### 4. Bar transfers need proof (`components/management/till.tsx` + the POS payment action)
**Now:** choose Transfer → Confirm paid. No reference, no check. Fake transfer alerts are the most common counter fraud at Nigerian bars and lounges.
**Want:** Transfer requires (a) the last 4 characters of the transfer reference or the sender's name, and (b) a tick: "I've checked the hotel's bank app and the money is there. A screenshot or SMS alert is not enough." (match the front desk wording on Take payment). Store the reference on the payment. Add a transfers-by-salesperson list to sales reports for the manager to check against the bank statement.

### 5. Blind cash count at daily closing (`app/(management)/app/sales/closing/page.tsx`)
**Now:** "Cash expected ₦6,500" is shown above the "Cash you counted" field.
**Want:** hide expected cash until the count is submitted. Then show expected, counted and over/short; if not zero, require a reason before closing. Managers still see everything at sign-off. Apply the same to any front desk cash count.
Also label the period: "Business day Tue 24 Nov (ends 06:00)" — a sale at 01:25 on 25 Nov sits in Tue 24 Nov's closing, which confuses staff.

### 6. Booking step 3 total ignores extras (`components/booking/guest-details-form.tsx`)
Ticking airport pickup + breakfast leaves "Total ₦40,000"; step 4 says ₦75,000. Recalculate the total and summary live as extras are ticked (per-night extras × nights), using the same pricing function as the server so they can't disagree.

### 7. Review page breaks words on phones (`app/(public)/book/review/[token]/page.tsx:62`)
`break-all` on the `<dd>` splits "Thu 26 Nov", "₦10,000", the email mid-word at 375px. Use `break-words` / `overflow-wrap:anywhere`; keep `break-all` only for the email.

### 8. Hall bookings (`components/management/hall-bookings.tsx`, `app/(management)/app/events`)
- A hall booking can be "Confirmed" with no price and no payment. Confirmed must mean paid in full, as for rooms; until then it's an Enquiry / Awaiting payment.
- After a validation error ("The hall is already booked at that time.") every field is wiped. Keep the values (return them in the action state or use `defaultValue`).

### 9. VAT on bar receipts (`components/management/pos-receipt.tsx`)
The receipt prints "Includes VAT (7.5%)". VAT is out of scope (Schedule B). Put it behind a setting that is off by default; I'll confirm with the hotel whether they're VAT-registered.
