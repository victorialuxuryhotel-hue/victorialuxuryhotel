Layout rules

  • One filled (primary) button per area: the next thing to do. Everything else is secondary or a text button.
  • The primary action sits where the eye ends: bottom of the order panel on the till, right end of a row elsewhere.
  • Pick the control by job: segmented for one choice that changes the form, tabs to switch views, chips to filter, a dropdown for long lists.
  • Money is always tabular, right-aligned in tables, and largest where it matters (the total, the change).
  • Status is a word in a coloured badge, never colour alone.
  • Badges, chips and buttons hug their text and never stretch to fill a box. Only the main button at the foot of a panel or form goes full width.
  • Under a full-width button, the links, notes and badges that go with it are centred on it, so the stack reads as one column.
  • Fields are as wide as what goes in them: a phone or amount is short, a name or note is long. Labels sit on top, left-aligned with the field.
  • Touch targets: 60px on the till, 44px in forms, 36px only inside table rows.
  • Group, then list: sections with a heading and a count beat one long list of equal rows.
