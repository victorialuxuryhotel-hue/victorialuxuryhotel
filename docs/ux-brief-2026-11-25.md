# UX brief — screens built around the working day

Companion to `docs/fix-brief-2026-11-25.md`. **Do the fix brief first**: the rules there (occupancy from check-in, pay before room, transfer proof, blind count) are the foundations these screens sit on. This brief reshapes the screens so each role works from one list through the day.

Full reasoning and research: the "Victoria Hotel screens: designed around the working day" doc. Read `AGENTS.md` and the glossary wording rules before starting.

## How the day runs (design for this, not for modules)

| Time | Front desk | Bar | Manager |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start of shift | Who's leaving, who owes, what did the last shift leave? | Float right? What's out? | Did yesterday close cleanly? |
| Morning | Check-outs, payments owed, rooms to cleaning | Restock | Approve refunds, yesterday's numbers |
| 12:00–14:00 | Today's arrivals paid? Rooms clean? | Lunch | |
| 14:00–20:00 | Take payment, key, fast | Evening starts | Anything stuck? |
| 19:00–23:00 | Late arrivals, walk-ins | **Rush** — speed, no lost tabs | |
| Close | Who didn't arrive / didn't leave? Count cash | Count cash, close | Sign off closings |

## Rules for every screen

1. One home screen per role, and it is a **work list** ("what needs me now"). Record lists (all bookings, all orders) are for lookup.
2. Status comes from recorded events, never from dates. A passed date with nothing recorded is a job on the work list.
3. One next action per item, in step order. Payment first. Never two jobs for one guest.
4. Every count equals the rows of the list it sits on. Test this.
5. At the counter, nothing important below the fold on the real device.
6. Undo for everyday actions; confirm only for money or irreversible actions.
7. Any cash count is blind.
8. Glossary words everywhere (Booking, Take payment, Counter, Till). Add a test that fails the build on banned words if one doesn't exist.

## Front desk

### Today — `app/(management)/app/today/page.tsx`, `components/management/today.tsx`, `domain/today.ts`

Make Today the only screen needed on a normal shift.

**Needs you now** — one row per guest, one button, in this order:
1. In house and owes money → Take payment
2. Overdue check-out (`checked_in`, check-out < today) → Check out / Change dates
3. Didn't arrive (`confirmed`, check-in < today) → Mark no-show / Still coming
4. Arriving today, not paid → Take payment
5. Arriving today, paid, room not clean → "Room 204 being cleaned" → Mark clean
Empty state: "Nothing needs you right now."
Never list the same guest twice; show the earliest unfinished step only.

**Below, in day order:** Leaving today (overdue first, warning style, balance shown) → Arriving today (paid? room? clean? one button) → In house (name, room, check-out date, balance, status — drop the source column and payment bars here).

**Rooms right now:** Ready / Occupied / To clean / Out of order. Each links to the board filtered. The four must add up to total rooms; Occupied must equal In house.

Tests: `domain/today.test.ts` cases for each item type, ordering, de-duplication per guest, counts.

### Booking page — `components/management/reservation-detail.tsx`, `reservation-guide.tsx`, `reservation-actions.tsx`

- Show only the actions for the current step. Awaiting payment → Take payment + Cancel only (no room picker, no Change room).
- Move "next step" logic out of `NextStepBar` into the domain (one function: booking → next action) so Today and the booking page can't disagree. Unit-test it.
- One money line under the name: "Total ₦75,000 · Paid ₦0 · Owes ₦75,000".
- Order: money → stay → guest → history.
- Unsuccessful online attempts collapsed: "Show 2 unsuccessful online attempts".
- Room picker: only clean and free rooms selectable; others greyed with the reason ("Occupied · Grace Adeyemi", "Being cleaned", "Out of order").

### Room board — `components/management/room-board.tsx`, `room-tile.tsx`, `server/queries/rooms.ts`

- Statuses: Occupied, Due out, Arriving, Ready, To clean, Out of order — from check-in/check-out + cleaning, never dates alone.
- Tile: number, status word, guest surname, one action. Tap = the obvious action; long-press/secondary = details.
- Check-out sets the room to To clean automatically.
- Must work well at 375px (marking rooms clean from the corridor).

### New booking — `app/(management)/app/reservations/new`, `reservation-form.tsx`

One short flow: phone → name → dates + room type with live price → Take payment → room auto-assigned → Check in. Target: a walk-in in under a minute. Format money (₦75,000, not 75000).

### Take payment — `offline-payment-form.tsx`

Keep the "money is in the hotel's account; a screenshot is not enough" tick. Add the transfer reference as required for transfers. Format the amount.

### Payments, Guests, Hall

- Payments: takings by method at the top; only received money in the list.
- Guests: search first; guest page shows stays and payments.
- Hall: upcoming list + one form; Confirmed only when paid; keep fields after an error.

### Close the day (new) — front desk

End of evening shift, three checks before the shift can close:
1. Cash in drawer — blind count, then over/short with a reason if not zero.
2. Didn't-arrive bookings → no-show or still coming.
3. Overdue check-outs → checked out or extended.
Record it like the bar closing (immutable, signed by the person, visible to managers).

## Restaurant & bar salesperson

### Till — `components/management/till.tsx`

Three fixed zones; only the menu scrolls. Must fit a 1280×800 laptop and a 1024×768 tablet with no page scroll.

| Left | Middle | Right (pinned) |
|---|---|---|
| Open orders, searchable by table/name, running total each, "+ New" at top | Counter switch, categories, item buttons with price; sold out greyed; "5 left" when low; favourites row (top 8 sellers for the counter) | This order: items +/−, total, table/name, **Pay**. Pay expands Cash / Transfer / POS in place |

- Transfer: reference (required) + "checked in the bank app" tick (from the fix brief).
- After payment: slim receipt panel with Print, focus returns to a new order. No full-page receipt.
- Keep "Your sales today · Cash to hand in" in the header.
- Phone (375px): stack as order → menu, with a sticky bottom bar "3 items · ₦6,500 · Pay".

### Orders & receipts, Daily closing
- Show the transfer reference on transfer sales.
- Closing: blind count (fix brief). Label "Business day Tue 24 Nov · ends 06:00".

## Restaurant & bar manager (walk the screens first; these are not yet tested)

Home = work list:
1. Closings waiting for sign-off (over/short in warning style)
2. Voids and cancellations today, with reasons
3. Low and out-of-stock items
4. Transfers today by salesperson (to check against the bank)
5. Today's sales by counter and payment method

Menu: add, price, mark sold out — usable on a phone. Stock (`components/management/stock.tsx`): clean list, one Record button per item (receive / waste / count); show the gap between sold (till) and counted.

## GM, hotel manager, owner — Dashboard `app/(management)/app/page.tsx` (walk first)

Phone first. Order:
1. **Anything wrong?** Refunds awaiting approval, cash short at any closing, money owed in house, overdue check-outs, rooms out of order — each a link to act.
2. **Today:** occupancy, arrivals/departures, money in by department (rooms, hall, restaurant, bar) and method.
3. **Trend:** this week / month vs last.
Drop "Recent bookings" from the dashboard (it's in Bookings).

## Finance (read-only)

One screen: money in by day and method across all departments, closings with over/short, refunds approved and paid, Excel export. No buttons that change data.

## Across all screens

- Idle lock + persistent "Signed in as <name> · Switch user" strip on shared desks (`staff-card.tsx`). No PIN switching yet.
- "Note for the next shift" on Today and the Till, shown to whoever signs in next; stored with author and time.
- Money always formatted; dates include the year when not the current year.
- Empty states say what to do next.
- Check every screen at 375px, the Till at 1024×768 and 1280×800.
