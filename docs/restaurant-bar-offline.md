# Restaurant & bar: when the internet is down

The till needs the internet. If the connection drops while you are serving, nothing already saved is lost, and a payment you tap twice is never recorded twice. If the connection stays down, keep serving with paper slips and enter them later.

## Before you need it

- Keep a book of **numbered paper slips** (with a carbon copy) at each counter.
- The restaurant & bar manager keeps track of which slip numbers were handed out.

## While the internet is down

1. Write each order on the next slip:
   - slip number, date and time
   - each item, how many, and the price
   - the total
   - how the customer paid: cash, transfer or POS
   - your name
2. Take payment as normal, and give the customer the top copy.
3. Keep the carbon copy and do not throw away a spoiled slip. Write "VOID" on it instead.

## When the internet is back

1. On the till, enter each slip as an order, in slip-number order.
2. In **Table or customer**, type the slip number, for example `Slip 0123`.
3. Take payment with the **same method** the customer used.
4. The receipt shows the time you entered it, not the time of sale. The slip number links the two.

## At closing

- Enter every slip before you close your day. Your cash count must include the cash taken on slips.
- The manager checks the slip numbers against the orders marked `Slip …` before signing off the closing.
